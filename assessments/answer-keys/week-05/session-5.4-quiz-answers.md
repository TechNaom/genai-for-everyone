# Session 5.4 Quiz Answers

---

## Q1: Allocation Bias
**Answer:** B) Loan approval denies 40% of women's applications vs. 20% of men's
- Allocation bias = denying resources/opportunities unfairly

---

## Q2: Demographic Parity
**Answer:** B) All groups get equal outcome rates
- Demographic parity = same approval/denial rates across groups

---

## Q3: Amazon Hiring Bot
**Answer:** B) Training data reflected 10 years of male-dominated hiring
- Historical bias → model bias. Bias isn't intentional; it's systematic.

---

## Q4: Fairness Trade-offs
**Answer:** D) All of the above
- Can't optimize for all fairness metrics simultaneously
- Trade-offs are inevitable; choose based on context

---

## Q5: Valid Mitigations
**Answer:** C) Ignore bias and hope it goes away
- Not valid. Real mitigations: oversample, fairness constraints, thresholds, transparency

---

## Q6: Model Card
**Answer:** C) Accuracy per group + limitations + recommended use
- Model cards document performance, fairness, limitations, appropriate use
