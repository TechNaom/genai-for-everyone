# Quiz — Session 1.1: What GenAI Actually Is (and Isn't)

_6 questions, mixed format. Answer key in `assessments/answer-keys/`._

**1. (Multiple choice)** Which of the following is a *generative* AI task?
A) Predicting whether a loan applicant will default
B) Sorting support tickets into "billing," "technical," "other"
C) Writing a first draft of a cover letter from a job description
D) Estimating a house's sale price from its features

**2. (Short answer)** In one or two sentences, explain the core mechanism by which a large language model produces text. Avoid the word "thinks."

**3. (Multiple choice)** Your phone's predictive text keyboard and a large language model both work on the same fundamental principle. What is it?
A) Both search a database of pre-written responses
B) Both predict the most likely next piece of text given what came before
C) Both use the same training data
D) Both are equally accurate

**4. (Short answer)** A coworker says: "ChatGPT told me the answer with total confidence, so it must be right." What's the flaw in this reasoning, based on what you learned today?

**5. (Scenario)** Your manager says: "Let's add AI to our checkout flow to stop fraud." Is this more likely a predictive AI problem or a generative AI problem? Justify your answer in one sentence.

**6. (Scenario)** A startup pitches a tool that "uses AI to write personalized birthday emails to customers automatically." Classify this as predictive or generative, and describe what the input and output would look like.
