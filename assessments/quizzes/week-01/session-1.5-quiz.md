# Quiz — Session 1.5: Limitations, Hallucination & Bias

_6 questions, mixed format. Answer key in `assessments/answer-keys/`._

**1. (Multiple choice)** What is hallucination, in the GenAI sense?
A) The model refusing to answer a question
B) The model generating fabricated content with the same fluent confidence as accurate content
C) The model crashing or producing an error
D) The model asking a clarifying question

**2. (Short answer)** Why doesn't a model's confidence level tell you anything reliable about whether its answer is correct?

**3. (Multiple choice)** Which of the following is NOT one of the bias entry points discussed in this chapter?
A) Training data
B) Prompt phrasing
C) Few-shot example selection
D) The model's server location

**4. (Short answer)** A coworker says "hallucination will basically be solved once we upgrade to the newest model." How would you respond, based on this chapter?

**5. (Scenario)** You're reviewing a model-generated answer that cites "a 2017 study by Dr. James Holloway showing 64.3% improvement." You can't find this study anywhere. What's the most likely explanation, and what signal tipped you off?

**6. (Scenario)** Your company's new AI feature confidently told a customer something false about your product. Write 2-3 sentences explaining what likely happened, in language appropriate for a non-technical stakeholder, without promising it will "never happen again."
