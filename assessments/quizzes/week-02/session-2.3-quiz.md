# Quiz — Session 2.3: Prompting Techniques II

_6 questions, mixed format. Answer key in `assessments/answer-keys/`._

**1. (Multiple choice)** Why does chain-of-thought prompting tend to improve accuracy on multi-step problems?
A) It makes the model run on more powerful hardware
B) Intermediate reasoning steps become part of the context that later steps are predicted from
C) It forces the model to search the internet for the answer
D) It always produces a shorter response

**2. (Short answer)** What is the honest limit of chain-of-thought prompting? Why doesn't visible reasoning guarantee a correct conclusion?

**3. (Multiple choice)** Step-back prompting is most valuable for which kind of task?
A) Simple factual lookups
B) Comparisons and recommendations with an underlying general framework
C) Translating a sentence
D) Formatting a list

**4. (Short answer)** What does it mean if running self-consistency on the same prompt 5 times produces 5 different answers? What should you do with that information?

**5. (Scenario)** Your team is deciding whether to apply self-consistency (running every prompt 5 times) to a high-volume, low-stakes feature like generating short product tags. What would you push back on, and why?

**6. (Scenario)** A teammate shows you a beautifully formatted, step-by-step chain-of-thought response and says "it must be right, look how thorough it is." How would you respond?
