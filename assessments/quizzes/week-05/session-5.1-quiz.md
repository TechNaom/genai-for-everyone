## Question 4: Regression Testing

Your prompt v1 scores 85% on accuracy. You improve it to v2. After testing, v2 scores 90% accuracy but only 70% safety (v1 was 90% safety).

What should you do?

A) Promote v2 (higher accuracy is more important)  
B) Stick with v1 (safety regression is unacceptable)  
C) Analyze the trade-off and decide based on your use case  
D) Test on more examples first  

**Answer:** C (but D is also reasonable)
