# Session 5.4 Quiz: Responsible AI & Bias

**6 questions. MC and short answer.**

---

## Q1: Types of Bias

Which is an example of **allocation bias**?

A) Training data has 90% male engineers  
B) Loan approval denies 40% of women's applications vs. 20% of men's  
C) Model learns "nurse" = female  
D) Model accuracy is 95% for English speakers  

**Answer:** B

---

## Q2: Measuring Fairness

**Demographic parity** means:

A) All groups have equal representation in training data  
B) All groups get equal outcome rates  
C) Model predictions equally accurate for all groups  
D) Similar individuals treated similarly  

**Answer:** B

---

## Q3: Real Incident

Amazon's hiring bot was biased because:

A) It was intentionally designed to discriminate  
B) Training data reflected 10 years of male-dominated hiring  
C) Bias is unavoidable in ML  
D) The company didn't care about fairness  

**Answer:** B

---

## Q4: Fairness Trade-offs

Optimizing for demographic parity might hurt:

A) Equal opportunity  
B) Calibration  
C) Individual fairness  
D) All of the above  

**Answer:** D

---

## Q5: Mitigations

Which is NOT a valid bias mitigation?

A) Oversample underrepresented groups  
B) Add fairness constraints to training  
C) Ignore bias and hope it goes away  
D) Use different thresholds per group  

**Answer:** C

---

## Q6: Model Card

A model card should document:

A) Overall accuracy only  
B) Accuracy only for majority group  
C) Accuracy per group + limitations + recommended use  
D) Anything the model does well  

**Answer:** C
