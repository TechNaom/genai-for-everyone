_5-8 questions, mixed format. Answer key in `assessments/answer-keys/`._

1. What are the four questions a good capstone scope must answer concretely?
2. Why is "an AI assistant that helps with everything at my job" not an acceptable capstone scope?
3. True or False: A strong capstone should be "just a chatbot" so it's simple to demo and explain.

5. Why is explicitly stating what's *out of scope* in a proposal as important as stating what's in scope?

7. A manager tells a new hire "build us an AI feature" with no further detail. What's the highest-leverage first move, according to this session?
