# Quiz — Session 7.5: Mock Technical Interviews

_5-8 questions, mixed format. Answer key in `assessments/answer-keys/`._

1. What are the four elements of a strong "walk me through your project" answer?
2. Why is "I used LangChain and a vector database" a weaker interview answer than describing the problem it solved and why you chose that chunking strategy?
3. True or False: A system design question about "design a GenAI feature for X" is primarily testing whether you know the right API calls.
4. **Scenario:** An interviewer asks you to design a customer support AI feature. What should be among your very first questions back to them, before describing any architecture?
5. You're given an eval report showing 60% accuracy and asked to diagnose why. What should you do before proposing a fix?
6. Why is "that was good" considered weak feedback in a mock interview setting? Give an example of what better feedback looks like.
7. What is a live-debugging interview question actually testing, according to this session?
